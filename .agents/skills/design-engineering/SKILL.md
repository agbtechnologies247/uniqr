# Design Engineering & Micro-Interactions (by Emil Kowalski Principles)

## Key Guidelines
1. **Fluid Spring Transitions**: Use `transition-all duration-200 ease-out` and spring transforms (`scale-[1.02]`, `active:scale-[0.98]`).
2. **Tactile Interactive Feedback**: Combine micro-shifts in background color, border glow (`shadow-cyan-glow`), and sound/haptic cues on action buttons.
3. **Ambient Backlight & Glow Halos**: Add soft blurred ambient halos (`blur-2xl opacity-40`) behind main cards and hero elements to simulate physical light reflection.
4. **Skeleton & Loading Polish**: Smooth shimmer keyframes (`bg-gradient-to-r from-slate-900 via-slate-800 to-slate-900`).
